Architectural Feet ↔ Decimal Feet Toolkit 1/16" fidelity

Designed for field engineers and survey teams. Parse architectural strings, normalize runs, and report engineering-ready decimal feet.
Resolution: 1/16 in

Architectural Input Parser

—
Accepts feet/inch marks, hyphenation, Unicode quotes, fractions (e.g., 5'-11 7/16"), and decimal feet.

Ⅰ. Feet & Inches → Decimal Feet

—
Formula: decimal_ft = feet + (inches + sixteenths/16) / 12
Tip: Use Normalize to auto-carry 16/16 → 1" and 12" → 1'. Example: 5 ft, 14 in, 20/16 → 6 ft, 3 in, 4/16.

Ⅱ. Decimal Feet → Feet & Inches (nearest 1/16")

—
Rounding: nearest 1/16" (handles carry: 11 15/16" + 1/16" → next foot).
Negatives apply to the entire run (e.g., −1.25 ft → −1' 3"). Decimal feet sync back to the left-hand form.

Field Notes & Operational Guidance

  • Architectural parser normalizes to feet/inches/sixteenths automatically and syncs both calculators.
  • Precision control: choose decimal places (2–6). Default is 3.
  • Keyboard-forward: Tab through fields, press Enter to convert. Copy buttons push to clipboard.
