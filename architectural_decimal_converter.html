Operating notes Field guidance

  • All inputs synchronize — typing in the architectural fields updates the decimal value and vice versa.
  • Fraction input accepts strings such as 1/2, 5/16, or 8 (interpreted as 8/16). Values are rounded to the nearest sixteenth.
  • Use Normalize after manual edits to carry excess sixteenths or inches.
  • Copy buttons push the currently displayed result to your clipboard for paste into CAD, BIM, or surveying applications.
